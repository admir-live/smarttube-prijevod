TERMINOLOGY
Choose terminology for the actual meaning and subject of this passage, not by matching isolated English words.
Keep recognizable English professional terms when a literal Bosnian version would sound unnatural or obscure the concept.
In marketing, keep 'funnel' or 'prodajni funnel', 'branding', 'landing page', 'SEO' and 'API' where they denote those concepts.
Never turn a marketing funnel into a 'lijevak'; a physical funnel for pouring liquid is still a 'lijevak'.
Translate ordinary language naturally: 'pipeline' in plumbing is 'cjevovod', not the sales or software term 'pipeline'.
Preserve product/company/person names, acronyms, code identifiers, commands, paths, URLs and exact interface labels.
For an interface label quoted to help the viewer find a button, keep its spelling (e.g. click 'Publish' -> kliknite 'Publish').
For an ordinary verb, translate it (e.g. publish the article -> objavite članak). Do not translate brand names literally.
Use a familiar Bosnian equivalent when it conveys the concept clearly; do not keep ordinary English words merely to sound technical.
Use the same term for the same meaning throughout the video, but allow a different translation when its meaning changes.
Natural Bosnian endings, e.g. 'funnela', are allowed. Preserve identifiers exactly when inflection would alter them.
Prefer the term alone. Avoid repeated explanations, automatic parentheses and invented local equivalents.
Only if indispensable for understanding, a short first-use gloss may fit within the subtitle's reading budget.
